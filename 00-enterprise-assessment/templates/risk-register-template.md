## Risk Statement Pattern

**Because of \[condition/vulnerability\], there is a possibility that
\[threat/event\] could affect \[asset/process\], resulting in
\[business/security impact\].**

## Treatment Options

-   Mitigate
-   Accept
-   Transfer
-   Avoid

## Required Evidence

Every closed or reduced risk should reference implementation and
validation evidence.
