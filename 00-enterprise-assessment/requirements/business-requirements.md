# Business Requirements

**Organization:** AFG Enterprises (modeled)  
**Phase:** 0 — Enterprise Assessment

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Success Measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-001 | Cloud governance must scale as subscriptions and workloads grow. | Critical | New subscriptions inherit approved platform controls. |
| BR-002 | Security controls must reduce risk without unnecessarily blocking delivery. | High | Documented exception and remediation paths. |
| BR-003 | Resource ownership and cost accountability must be discoverable. | High | Required ownership metadata coverage ≥95%. |
| BR-004 | Critical workloads must have documented resilience objectives. | High | Tier 1 services have approved RTO/RPO. |
| BR-005 | Architecture decisions must be auditable and explainable. | High | Significant decisions have ADRs. |
| BR-006 | The platform must support hybrid workloads during modernization. | High | Azure-native and Arc governance patterns defined. |
| BR-007 | Engineering teams must have repeatable secure deployment patterns. | High | Approved IaC/pipeline templates available. |
| BR-008 | Cloud security must support future AI adoption. | Medium-High | AI baseline established before broad production adoption. |
| BR-009 | The enterprise must be able to measure governance effectiveness. | High | Executive/security governance metrics defined. |
| BR-010 | Modernization must be incremental rather than dependent on a greenfield rebuild. | Critical | Legacy migration path documented. |
